# Brand Showcase image generation guide

The Brand Showcase section expects **seven WebP assets** under:

```text
public/images/brand-showcase/
```

The interface already provides stable placeholders, final aspect ratios, and safe fallback behavior. Add files using the exact filenames below; no component change is required.

## Global asset rules

- Export as WebP with high visual quality.
- Do not include product names, brand wordmarks, prices, badges, buttons, product counts, or marketing copy in the image.
- Do not add watermarks or third-party logos.
- Use fictional product styling rather than copying a protected commercial design exactly.
- Keep edges clean and suitable for light or dark UI surfaces.
- Avoid excessive empty transparent padding.
- Use consistent premium studio lighting across the collection.
- Keep the product fully inside the canvas safe area.


**Recommended canvas:** 1800 × 1500 px or larger, near 6:5.

**Composition:** Two premium fictional smartphones in metallic navy/titanium blue. Show one rear view with a sophisticated camera system and one front view with an abstract electric-blue display.

**Positioning:** Devices should occupy approximately 82–88% of the canvas, weighted slightly toward the lower center. Preserve a 5–7% edge safe area.

**Lighting:** Cool blue rim light, polished metal reflections, realistic dark contact shadow.

**Gemini direction:**

> Premium studio render of two fictional flagship smartphones, metallic midnight-blue titanium finish, one rear three-camera view and one front display view, elegant electric-blue abstract screen glow, clean transparent background, cinematic cool rim lighting, realistic polished materials, large product composition filling 85 percent of canvas, no text, no logo, no badge, no watermark.
